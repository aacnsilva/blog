How a month works

Plan it, log it, settle it.

01 — Plan

Set the month once.

Add each person’s usual income and the costs that repeat: rent, internet, electricity. Justa works out who covers what. Change one month without touching the rest.

02 — Log

Extras, as they happen.

Groceries, Friday dinner, the pharmacy. Add it in a few taps, or photograph the receipt. The text is read on the iPhone, never uploaded.

03 — Settle

One line at month end.

Justa nets every shared extra against each person’s share. No spreadsheets, no “I think I paid last time”. Just who pays whom, and how much.

Questions

Good to know.

Why split by income instead of 50/50?

If one person earns 55% of the household, covering 55% of the shared plan leaves both with the same share of what they earn. You can still set any split you like for a single month.

How do two iPhones share a ledger?

Create a vault in an empty folder on iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Google Drive and share that folder. The second iPhone opens the same folder. If both edit the same bill at the same time, the later save wins.

Is the app in English?

Justa follows your iPhone’s language, in English or European Portuguese. Amounts use the currency of the household.

What do you collect?

Nothing. See the privacy policy.
