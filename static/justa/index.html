Questions

Good to know.

Why split by income instead of 50/50?

If one person earns 55% of the household, covering 55% of the shared plan leaves both with the same share of what they earn. You can still give someone a percent or a fixed amount, split any extra 50/50, or set custom shares.

How do two iPhones share a household?

With Justa Plus, tap Start sharing and choose an empty folder in iCloud Drive, Dropbox or Google Drive, then share that folder. On the second iPhone, tap Join a shared household (free) and pick the same folder. If you both edit the same bill at once, the latest save is kept.

Does a receipt photo leave my iPhone?

No. Justa reads the text on this iPhone with Apple Vision, or straight from a PDF’s text layer. The file is never uploaded or saved. Only the fields you keep — title, amount, date, category, note — go into the household book.

Can we each have our own theme?

Yes. Themes stay on each iPhone, so one of you can live in Azulejo while the other falls asleep to Fado.

Is the app in English?

Justa follows your iPhone’s language, in English or European Portuguese. Amounts use the currency of the household.

What do you collect?

Nothing. See the privacy policy.
